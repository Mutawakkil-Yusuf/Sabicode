# Code of conduct

Sabicode is a learning project. The people who use it are learners, teachers,
and volunteers. Treat them with respect.

- Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- No shaming someone for their English, their Pidgin, their code, or their
  experience level.
- Assume good faith, but take responsibility when you get it wrong.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code. Repeated or serious violations can lead to a ban from the project.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer on GitHub:
<https://github.com/Mutawakkil-Yusuf>. Reports are handled privately.
